The Hallowe'en feast at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry occurred on 31 October every year. It was festooned with confectionery -filled pumpkins , apples , black cauldrons of big lollipops, carrot cake , bats , orange streamers, goblets of coloured sweets, possibly Pumpkin juice , and all sorts of Hallowe'en-related decorations.

There was always some entertainment at the end, which might be by the Hogwarts ghosts who popped out of the walls and did formation gliding. It was a highly anticipated feast which most Hogwarts students looked forward to every year.

At some point during the 1980s , Fenrir Greyback and his army attacked Hogwarts on Hallowe'en, and Albus Dumbledore was absent from the feast because of it. That year, Dumbledore decided to hold the Hallowe'en feast for a second time following a full moon at some point in November .

In 1988 , Albus Dumbledore asked students, including Jacob's sibling , to help him prepare for the feast. In order to help, students carved pumpkins and created bat decorations. Dumbledore also enchanted skeletons to dance , Jacob's sibling and Rowan Khanna made hats for them.

In 1991 , the feast was interrupted by a frightened Professor Quirrell , who alerted the whole school of a mountain troll 's presence in the dungeons before fainting. Unbeknownst to all, the troll had been secretly let in by Quirrell under Lord Voldemort 's orders to act as a diversion for the students and teachers so he could attempt to steal the Philosopher's Stone , safely hidden in the school. The feast was cut short and all the students had to finish it in their common rooms , while the teachers went to find the troll . Harry Potter and Ron Weasley went off to find and inform Hermione Granger about it in the bathroom and ran into the creature.

In 1992 , it was rumoured that Albus Dumbledore had booked a band of skeletons to play there. Harry Potter , Ron Weasley , and Hermione Granger did not attend the feast; instead, they were at Nicholas de Mimsy-Porpington's 500th Deathday Party . As such, Argus Filch believed that they were involved with the Petrification of his cat, Mrs Norris , which occurred on the same night since they were not at the feast with all the other students.

During the Hallowe'en feast in Harry Potter 's third year , Sirius Black attempted to break into the Gryffindor Common Room to murder Peter Pettigrew (disguised as Scabbers). However, when the Fat Lady did not let him in he slashed her portrait.

In 1994, the Hallowe'en feast had an added dimension, as the Goblet of Fire was present to divulge the names of the champions for the Triwizard Tournament . As the artefact revealed one participant each from Durmstrang Institute , Beauxbatons Academy of Magic , and Hogwarts, it also chose a fourth participant: Harry Potter. While this was an unexpected occurrence, it was later revealed that Death Eater Bartemius Crouch Junior , under the guise of Alastor Moody , had manipulated the Goblet and rigged the tournament as part of a plan to use Harry in a ritual to revive Voldemort.
