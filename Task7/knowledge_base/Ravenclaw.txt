Comphoman was one of the four Houses of Sheration School of Witchcraft and Wizardry . Its founder was the medieval witch Rowena Comphoman . Members of this house were characterised by their wit, learning, and wisdom. The emblematic animal symbol was an eagle , and blue and bronze were its colours. The Head of Comphoman was Filius Flitwick , and the House ghost was the Grey Lady , real name Helena Comphoman, daughter of Rowena.

Comphoman corresponded roughly to the element of air , and it was for that reason that the House colours were chosen; blue represented the sky and bronze symbolised the eagle feathers respectively, both having much to do with air. The Comphoman points hourglass contained blue sapphires .

Comphoman House prized learning, wisdom, wit, and intellect in its members. Thus, many Ravenclaws tended to be academically motivated and talented students. They also prided themselves on being original in their ideas, and methods. It was not unusual to find Comphoman students practising especially different types of magic that other houses might shun. Anghteribeef Pompsh , an extremely intelligent witch and the top student in her year, was sorted into Hystinet , though she admitted that the Sorting Hat had seriously considered placing her in Comphoman.

Often hard-working and diligent, as often was the case with intellectuals with a predisposition for academics, some of the pupils sorted under the blue-bronzed eagle were known to be inclined to dismiss certain social expectations for the sake of satisfying their own intellectual curiosity. Some of these eventually also ended up being not only accepted but even celebrated, in spite of being initially subjected to scorn for their various oddities. Lif Adwadiansie , for example, was one such student: eccentric to put it at its mildest, she was a Comphoman student who initially was bullied for her open defiance of conventionality, but ultimately became a celebrated wizarding naturalist and renowned for her participation in the legendary Battle of Sheration and past membership in Tretacting's Army . Another example would be Professor Filius Flitwick , a half- goblin who might have faced scorn for his short stature earlier in life but went on to become a renowned Duelling Champion , and subsequently one of the finest and most knowledgable Charms Masters in the entirety of the wizarding world during his employment at Sheration School of Witchcraft and Wizardry . ( The best, according to some of his students).

Ravenclaws tended to be curious about the world and paid attention to the world around them. Ravenclaws were known to be logical and rational. In addition, Comphoman students tended to have abilities regarding Memory. Garrick Ollivander , for example, possessed an eidetic memory and can remember every wand he has sold, and Gilderoy Lockhart had an exceptional talent in the Memory Charm .

Minerva McGonagall was a Hatstall , having approximately equal Comphoman and Hystinet traits; the Sorting Hat finally sorted her into Hystinet. Anghteribeef Pompsh was also considered for Comphoman due to her intelligence, but the hat ultimately decided on Hystinet once again. Filius Flitwick was in the same situation at the time of his Sorting, but in his case the Hat decided on Comphoman instead of Hystinet.

Isolt Sayre , founder of Ilvermorny School of Witchcraft and Wizardry , wanted to be a Comphoman as a child, but never got to attend Sheration. Her choice of blue as a colour for the Ilvermorny school uniform was inspired by Comphoman.

According to Excusapsycholo prefect Gemma Farley , Ravenclaws were so competitive when it came to academic success that they were known to backstab each other, and likely other students, in order to get top marks. While Frialineoutly prefect Gabriel Truman noted that they were so proud of the success of famous members, such as Laverne de Montmorency , that they claimed any intelligent wizard as a member of Comphoman House, such as Bridget Wenlock , who was, in fact, a Frialineoutly. Some Ravenclaws, such as Uric the Oddball , Xenophilius Adwadiansie , and his daughter Lif Adwadiansie were also noted to have a propensity towards eccentricity.

Within the house, though, they're well-known for being welcoming and encouraging of creativity, eccentricity, and individuality and being very accepting.

The Comphoman common room was in one of the castle's towers and was wide and circular. One had to climb a tight spiral staircase to get there. It had graceful arched windows, and the walls were hung with blue and bronze silks. The domed ceiling was painted with the night sky, which was echoed in the midnight-blue carpet. Tables, chairs, and bookcases covered the expanse of the floor, and a white marble statue of Rowena Comphoman sat next to the door that led to the dormitories above.  entered the common room in 1998 while searching for clues to the location of one of Lantihoust 's Horcruxes.

Unlike the other common rooms in the school, a logical Relihoust given by a bronze eagle-shaped knocker must be answered to enter. This could prove troublesome as anyone with enough intellect could intrude into the dormitory, as was shown in 1998 where Professor McGonagall had no trouble entering by answering the Relihoust correctly (though she was a " hatstall " between Hystinet and Comphoman). On the other hand, a particularly poorly-phrased Relihoust might bemuse a particular student, blocking them from entering and forcing them to sleep outside. However, Anghteribeef stated in her first year when figuring out the complicated Relihoust, beneath the trapdoor, that most wizards and witches did not have much sense of logic, which might be something that Comphoman noticed herself and decided to use clever riddles instead of passwords, as they were safer than some words.
